# Content decisions

Scope: original Mega Man X, X2, X3 and X4. X4 has separate X/Zero pages; X3 does not cover the Zero Project hack. Legacy Collection generally preserves the stage mechanics; secret-start controls depend on platform and selected region.

## Inventory and routes

| Guide | Hearts | Health tanks | Armor slots | Other stage items |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| X1 | 8 | 4 | 4 | Hadouken separately |
| X2 | 8 | 4 | 4 | Three Zero parts and Shoryuken separately |
| X3 | 8 | 4 | 4 | Four Ride Armors; four mutually exclusive chip locations |
| X4 X | 8 | 2 | 4 | One Weapon Tank, one EX Tank |
| X4 Zero | 8 | 2 | 0 | One Weapon Tank, one EX Tank |

X1 uses the Penguin-first weakness chain. Octopus precedes Chameleon so the heart route is flooded; Eagle's helmet precedes Mammoth's arms. Planned cleanup: Penguin, Mandrill, Kuwanger. Armadillo's timing-dependent heart may need a retry. Hadouken is optional and not retained by ordinary passwords. Zero's X1 sacrifice is scripted.

X2 retains the established guide and its conservative pause after boss 2 to collect all three X-Hunter parts. This is a safe route checkpoint, not a claim that the parts are lost immediately after boss 3. Ordinary armor/heart/tank collection does not require restoring Zero.

X2 detour clarification: the red opening warning, boss-2 checkpoint and detour explain Game Over → Continue for leaving uncleared stages while preserving collected parts and the Maverick count. Pause → Exit is limited to previously cleared stages. The eight entrance/approach frames come from PrimeraEspada91's [All X Hunters Gate Locations](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7DorArmLbIg), with per-frame timestamp credits. They identify stage landmarks, not fixed hunter assignments. Crystal Snail's instructions now include clearing the sliding crystal and bringing the Ride Armor to the upper-left barrier.

X3 uses Buffalo → Seahorse → Rhino → Catfish → Crawfish → Tiger → Beetle → Hornet. Tiger's arms and Hornet's Chimera unlock the cleanup. The four pink chips are explicitly marked SKIP for Gold, excluded from the completion requirement and not included in return-trip collection lists. Gold requires all regular items, full health and no prior chip; ordinary passwords omit Gold.

The X3 saber route pauses after Tiger, when Ray Splasher and Spinning Blade are available. Finish unarmored Vile with one of these weapons before all eight Mavericks are defeated. Keep Zero alive; switch to him before Mosquitus in **Doppler Stage 2**. RetroPixel's item page incorrectly labels that stage as Stage 1; HonestGamers' dedicated Doppler 2 walkthrough and MMHP agree on Stage 2. Bit/Byte destruction is a different branch, not an extra saber prerequisite.

X4 campaigns have distinct stage orders, rewards, combat recommendations and Cyber Peacock S-rank room numbers. For X, rooms 1/2/3 reward heart/tank/helmet; for Zero they reward life/heart/tank. Two arm capsules are interchangeable alternatives for one slot. Zero has no collectible armor capsules. Ultimate Armor/Black Zero codes belong before New Game; Black Zero is cosmetic in the original console game.
